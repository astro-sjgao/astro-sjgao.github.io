Abstract
======

PSR J2032+4127 is the only gamma-ray binary that exhibits pulsations in gamma rays. Previous research has indicated that the pulsar and the Be star MT91 213 orbit each other in a highly eccentric orbit with an extremely long period, with the pulsar reaching its periastron on 2017 November 13. Since its launch, the Fermi satellite has been monitoring this pulsar for 16 yr, covering the 8 yr before and the 8 yr after the pulsar passed its periastron.
