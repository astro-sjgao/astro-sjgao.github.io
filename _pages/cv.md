Selected Research Highlights
======
* Discovered PSR J1750-3116A, the first radio pulsar likely associated with the globular cluster Terzan 6, using C-band GBT observations.
* Reported the discovery of 19 new pulsars from FAST archival observations, including long-period and sporadic sources recovered through FFA and single-pulse searches.
* Investigated RRAT J2325-0530 with FAST observations, showing an atypical waiting-time distribution and evidence for complex magnetospheric behavior.
* Studied possible formation channels for mass-gap black holes from neutron-star X-ray binaries under super-Eddington accretion.
